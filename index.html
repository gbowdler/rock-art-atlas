Sources & notes

    This atlas is a working, growing reference — sites are chosen to cross continents and surface genuinely interesting motif comparisons, not to be exhaustive. Dating for rock art is often contested and revised — ages above are given as reported ranges, not settled fact, and are worth re-checking against primary literature before you rely on them for anything beyond browsing. Coastlines are real (Natural Earth 110m resolution, public domain, via the world-atlas package), so they're accurate at world-map scale but simplified — not survey-grade.
